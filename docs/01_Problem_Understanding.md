# 01 — Problem Understanding

## The Challenge

Modern online learning platforms (Coursera, Udemy, YouTube, edX) host millions of hours of educational content. However, aspiring engineers face significant friction when trying to transition into high-growth technical roles (such as AI/ML Engineer, Data Scientist, or Cloud Architect):

1. **Information Overload without Direction**: Learners are bombarded with thousands of disconnected courses without knowing which skills actually move them closer to employability.
2. **Generic, One-Size-Fits-All Curricula**: Most learning platforms recommend courses based on popular tags or collaborative filtering rather than the learner's specific existing proficiency and identified skill gaps.
3. **Black-Box Course Recommendations**: Recommendation engines rarely explain *why* a course is suggested, leaving learners uncertain whether a 40-hour course is worth their limited weekly study budget.
4. **Disregard for Prerequisite Graphs**: Recommending advanced deep learning before linear algebra or statistics causes cognitive overload and high dropout rates.
5. **Static, Non-Adaptive Roadmaps**: When a learner struggles on a quiz or speeds through material, standard platforms fail to dynamically adjust the pacing, review material, or subsequent milestones.

## The Core Question

> **"What should I learn next to reach my career goal in the shortest, most effective path given my current skills and weekly time budget?"**

PathFinder was architected specifically to solve this problem by providing an intelligent, explainable, and adaptive career roadmap navigation system.
